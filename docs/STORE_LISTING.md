## Full description

> **Your voice. Your craft. A national storefront.**
>
> Karigar AI is an AI co-seller for India's artisans. Take one photo and speak
> one sentence in your own language — Karigar AI does the photography clean-up,
> the writing, the translation, the fair pricing, and the publishing.
>
> **What it does**
> • 📷 Studio-quality product photo from a workshop snap — background removed
>   and re-lit.
> • 🎙️ Voice-first in **9 Indian languages** — no typing, no English, no forms.
> • 🤖 A full listing written for you — title, description, materials, tags —
>   in English, Hindi, Kannada and your own language.
> • ✓ **Verified GI tags** — your Channapatna toy or Mysore silk is checked
>   against the real Geographical Indication registry.
> • 💰 A **fair price you can defend** — grounded in market comparables and a
>   fair-wage floor that never prices below your labour.
> • 🚀 **Publish once, reach every channel** — live on the ONDC network with a
>   shareable QR storefront.
> • 🛒 See your product the way a buyer does, across the network.
>
> Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26090). Works even offline.
