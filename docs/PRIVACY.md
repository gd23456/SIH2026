# Privacy Policy — Karigar AI

_Last updated: 2026-09-10. This is the canonical text; the app also shows it
in-app (Profile → Privacy policy). This is a hackathon prototype policy and
will be finalised before any public Play Store release._

## What we collect

- **Account:** your name, and — if you sign in — your Google email or phone
  number and profile photo (via Firebase Authentication).
- **Content:** the product photos, descriptions, prices and listings you create.
- **Basic technical data** needed to run the service (e.g. which mode the app
  is in). We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Why we collect it

- To create and secure your account.
- To generate your listings and publish them to **ONDC**.
- To show your catalogue back to you across devices.

## What we never do

- We never sell or rent your data.
- We **never ask for or store passwords** for any other marketplace.
- Channels shown in the app as **"demo"** (Meesho, Myntra, Amazon Karigar,
  Flipkart Samarth, WhatsApp Business) are **simulated** — no data is sent to
  them. Only **ONDC** is a real publish.

## AI processing

Listing text and price suggestions are generated by Google Gemini (free tier)
or, offline, by an on-device mock. Your photo may be sent to the image service
for background removal. No content is used to train third-party models by us.

## Your choices

- Edit your name/location in Profile at any time.
- Your photos and listings can be removed on request.
- Sign out clears the account from the device.

## Data retention

Listings persist so your storefront and catalogue keep working. Prototype data
lives in a local SQLite database on the operator's machine.

## Contact

For any data request or question, contact the Karigar AI team (SIH26090).
